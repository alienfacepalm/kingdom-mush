# Capkeep look shots

Gameplay stays on the flat field. These pictures are the stylized 3D read: chunky models, painterly textures, one fixed high three-quarter camera, no orbit.

`dewcap.png`, `glasscut.png`, `forkfen.png`, `spireyard.png`, and `blackcap.png` are the retuned marches. The `*-look.jpg`, `dewcap-march.jpg`, and `dewcap-towers.jpg` files are concept stills made from those screenshots. The road in `dewcap-look.jpg` is the Dust Basin shape to keep. `dewcap-march.jpg` is only the creature lineup.

## Shared direction

Fixed camera, high three-quarter, slight tilt, orthographic feel. Dirt track of constant width, shoulders of grass or stone, build pads beside the track, two red flags at the hold. Original mushroom creatures and towers. No interface, no letters, no copied maps.
